# First-attempt usability validation protocol

Status: protocol ready; representative-photographer sessions have not yet been conducted. Do not mark
T062 complete until anonymized observations from actual participants are recorded below.

## Purpose

Validate SC-002 and SC-003: a photographer unfamiliar with the app should complete the primary
calculator and reusable-equipment journeys correctly on the first attempt, without coaching.

## Participants and privacy

- Recruit at least five photographers spanning beginner, enthusiast, and professional experience.
- Do not collect names, account identifiers, exact locations, client information, or image libraries.
- Assign random participant IDs such as P01. Record only broad experience band and platform familiarity.
- Obtain consent for anonymous task timing and observation. Stop immediately if consent is withdrawn.

## Build and environment

- Record commit SHA, release artifact, phone model, OS version, display scaling, and enabled accessibility
  services.
- Install the persistently signed Android prerelease or an independently signed iOS device build.
- Disable network access before starting and begin with cleared application data.
- The facilitator may read the task prompt but must not identify controls or explain photography terms.

## Tasks

1. Create a camera with a 36 × 24 mm sensor and 0.03 mm circle of confusion.
2. Create a 24–70 mm f/2.8 lens and a 10-stop ND filter.
3. Use the camera and lens to calculate depth of field for 70 mm, f/8, focused at 10 m.
4. Explain the near limit, far limit, assumptions, and limitation shown by the app.
5. Calculate a 10-stop exposure from 1/30 second and identify when Bulb/timer guidance applies.
6. Save both results, add a note, archive the lens, switch units, restart offline, and reopen the original
   depth-of-field result.
7. Enable 200% text scaling or the participant's screen reader and reopen the saved result.

## Measures

For each task record success on first attempt, completion time, wrong turns, requests for help, visible
confusion, and whether the participant can accurately explain the result. SC-002 passes when at least 80%
complete the calculator journey without coaching. SC-003 passes when at least 80% create and reuse
equipment without coaching. Accessibility failures, clipped controls, misunderstood units, and unsafe
interpretations are release-blocking observations regardless of the aggregate percentage.
